Recordings
Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), by Versilian Studios LLC.
Folder: Chordophones/Zithers/Harpsichord, Flemish (Sustains/Low, the 8' register).
Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). No credit is required. Credit is given here anyway.
The recordings are supplied without warranties. They are not of the instrument in the pictures.

This pack includes one take for every whole tone from F#1 to C6. For Alto, each take was
trimmed to the pluck, cut to 1.6 seconds, level-matched, faded out, and converted to
32 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV. The filenames below identify the original takes.
